# How replies end

Applies to every session on this repo, laptop or cloud. Ryan reads these on a phone between other things.

- Plain British English, short. Lead with the outcome. No em dashes.
- Every PR, Actions run, release, commit or page you mention is a clickable markdown link with the full `https://` URL. Never a bare `#123` or a bare SHA on its own.
- When a turn finishes work, or stops because it needs Ryan, end with these three, in this order:
  1. **What you need to do**: numbered steps only Ryan can do, each with its link or the exact place to click. Or "Nothing."
  2. `Push status:` pushed and live / pushed, CI running / not pushed (and why), with the commit linked.
  3. `Still waiting on you:` one line, or "nothing."
- The echo-ponytail closing line (what was skipped, unchecked or risky) goes just above these three, never instead of them.
- Keep the desktop app's recap card: when its wrap-up tool is available (laptop sessions in the Claude desktop app), call it just before the final reply. Cloud sessions don't have it; the ending above still applies.
- A short progress note mid-task needs none of this.
