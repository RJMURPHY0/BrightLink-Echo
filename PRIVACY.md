# BrightLink Echo privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

BrightLink Echo is a Windows dictation app published by BRIGHTLINK (OS) LTD,
registered in England and Wales, company number 17459281. This policy covers
what the app does with your data.

## Your voice stays on your computer

Speech recognition runs on your own computer. Your recordings, the text of
your dictations, your vocabulary, snippets, learned phrases and settings are
saved in your Windows user folder. Your vocabulary, snippets and learned
phrases are also backed up to your BrightLink account when you are signed in
(see "Your words are always backed up" below). The text of your dictations and
your recordings stay on your computer, except for these:

- **The AI accuracy check**, which is on by default when you are signed in. It
  sends the text of a dictation, never the audio, to an AI provider to fix
  mistakes from context (see "AI features" below). You can switch it off in
  Settings.
- **Cloud Sync** (Settings > Account, off by default), described below.
- **Voice training**, if you turn it on.
- **A feedback report** from History, when you choose to send one (and its
  recording, when you tick "Include the recording").

## Your words are always backed up

When you are signed in, Echo backs up the words you teach it so a new or
reinstalled computer gets them back: your custom vocabulary, your snippets and
the phrases it has learned that you say often. This does not need Cloud Sync.
It never includes your history, your recordings, or the fragments of speech
Echo is still counting before it decides a phrase is worth learning. To remove
them, delete the entries in Echo (they are removed from your account on the
next backup) or ask for your account data to be deleted.

## Cloud Sync (off unless you turn it on)

Cloud Sync keeps your Echo data in your BrightLink account so it follows you
between computers. It is off on every computer until you switch it on there.
While it is on, these are stored in your account and copied to your other
computers that also have it on:

- The text of your dictations (your History) and their recordings
- The fragments of speech Echo counts to learn phrases
- Your preferences (not machine settings such as your microphone or hotkeys)

Turning Cloud Sync off stops it straight away, and Echo offers to delete
the history, recordings and preferences it has stored in your account. You can also delete it at any time
from Settings > Account > "Delete my data from the cloud". Copies on your own
computer are kept.

## What is always stored in your account

If you sign in, your account holds your name, email address and, if you give
one, your company name, plus daily usage figures (words dictated, time spoken,
refines used, days active) that power "Your impact" and your BrightLink
dashboards. These are numbers only, never what you said. Signing in is
optional: the app works offline without an account.

## AI features

Refine, Fix All and the AI accuracy check (on by default, Settings to switch
it off) send the text being
worked on (never audio) to an AI provider: OpenRouter, which routes to model
providers such as Google, or Anthropic. The text is used only to return the
result.

## Diagnostics

To find and fix faults, the app reports errors such as a failed text insert,
a silent microphone, a slow start or a crash. A report holds the app version,
timings, memory figures, the kind of microphone (for example "built-in
array" or "headset", never its name) and the kind of app you were typing into
(for example "browser" or "office", never its name). A crash report also
holds the last lines of the app's log with microphone names, your Windows
user name and email addresses taken out. A report never includes what you
said unless you have Cloud Sync on, when a short excerpt may be attached to a
transcription-quality report.

Reports are linked to your account so we can help you when something goes
wrong. We send them because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the app
working for you (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). They are deleted automatically
after 90 days. To stop them, switch off Settings > Account > Send
Diagnostics: no report leaves your computer while it is off.

## Who processes your data

Account data, usage figures, Cloud Sync data, feedback reports and
diagnostics reports are stored with Supabase, hosted in the EU. AI requests go to OpenRouter or Anthropic as
above. Updates are downloaded from GitHub, and the speech model from Hugging
Face. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Your rights

You can delete any dictation from History, and everything Cloud Sync stored
from Settings. With Cloud Sync on, a deleted dictation is removed from the
cloud straight away and from your other PCs when they next sync; Echo keeps
only its time and a one-way fingerprint of the words (never the words) so no
PC can upload it again. To get a copy of your data, have it corrected or have
your account deleted, email
[ryan.murphy@brightlink.io](mailto:ryan.murphy@brightlink.io). We reply
within 30 days.
