# BrightLink Echo privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

BrightLink Echo is a Windows dictation app published by BRIGHTLINK (OS) LTD,
registered in England and Wales, company number 17459281. This policy covers
what the app does with your data.

## Your voice stays on your computer

Speech recognition runs on your own computer. Recordings are saved in your
Windows user folder so you can replay them from History, and they are never
uploaded, with two exceptions that you switch on yourself:

- **Voice training**, if you turn it on.
- **A feedback report** from History, when you tick "Include the recording".

## What is stored in your account

If you sign in, these sync to your BrightLink account so they follow you
between computers:

- The text of your dictations (your History)
- Your custom vocabulary and snippets
- Usage figures shown under "Your impact" (words, time saved, days active)
- Feedback reports you send, including any correction you type

Your account holds your name, email address and, if you give one, your
company name. Signing in is optional: the app works offline without an
account.

## AI features

Refine, Fix All and the background accuracy check send the text being
worked on (never audio) to an AI provider: OpenRouter, which routes to model
providers such as Google, or Anthropic. The text is used only to return the
result.

## Diagnostics

To find and fix faults, the app reports errors such as a failed text insert
or a silent microphone. A report can include the name of the app you were
typing into, your microphone's name and memory figures. It never includes
what you said.

## What stays local only

Phrases the app learns from how you talk, your settings and your sign-in
session (encrypted with Windows' own data protection) stay on your computer.

## Who processes your data

Account data, History and reports are stored with Supabase, hosted in the
EU. AI requests go to OpenRouter or Anthropic as above. Updates are
downloaded from GitHub, and the speech model from Hugging Face. We do not
sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Your rights

You can delete any dictation from History. To get a copy of your data, have
it corrected or have your account deleted, email
[ryan.murphy@brightlink.io](mailto:ryan.murphy@brightlink.io). We reply
within 30 days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
